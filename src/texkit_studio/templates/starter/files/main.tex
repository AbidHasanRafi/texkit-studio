\section{Writing text}
Paragraphs are separated by a blank line. You can make text \textbf{bold},
\textit{italic} or \texttt{monospaced}. Press Ctrl+B or Ctrl+I (Cmd on a Mac)
to do it quickly.

\section{Maths}
Inline maths goes between dollar signs, like $E = mc^2$. Displayed equations
get a number you can refer to:
\begin{equation}
  \int_0^\infty e^{-x^2}\,dx = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2}.
  \label{eq:gauss}
\end{equation}
Equation~\eqref{eq:gauss} is the Gaussian integral.
